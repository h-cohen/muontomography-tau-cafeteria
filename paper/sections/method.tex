\section{From transmission to a room image}\label{sec:method}
For position $p$, opacity in a world angular bin was
\begin{equation}
\lambda_p=-\ln\!\left[\frac{n_p}{(t_p/t_{\mathrm{sky}})n_{\mathrm{sky}}}\right],\qquad
\sigma_{\lambda,p}^2\simeq\frac{1}{n_p}+\frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{sky}}},
\label{eq:opacity}
\end{equation}
where $n$ and $t$ are counts and live times. The common detector response cancels to the extent that it remained stable between runs. This normalization fixes the opacity zero point; unmeasured reference bins are masked, and the nominal inversion does not fit additive offsets. Figure~\ref{fig:opacity} shows the measured projections.

\caption{Opacity at the two room positions and open-sky reference counts. The broad background and repeated beam shadows are retained.}\label{fig:opacity}

The voxel model was $\boldsymbol\lambda=A\boldsymbol q$, with $q_j$ an effective opacity density and $A_{ij}$ the aperture-averaged path through voxel $j$. Uniform sub-rays sampled the ideal direction-conditioned coincidence footprint in the effective detector midpoint plane: its detector-coordinate widths were $W-|t_x|D$ and $W-|t_y|D$ for active width $W$ and effective layer separation $D$. The footprint rotated with detector azimuth. It was solved with nonnegative iterative updates and horizontal total variation \citep{gilbert1972,rudin1992}. Vertical smoothing was disabled to preserve parallax; coverage damping reduced poorly constrained edge artefacts. The $\ReconstructionSpacing\,\mathrm{m}$ voxel spacing describes the discretization, not isotropic spatial resolution. The solve volume covered the ray footprints, with cropping applied only for display.

The relative pose minimized cross-position prediction error on two half-voxel-shifted lattices. The fitted floor offset was $(\PoseX,\PoseY)\,\mathrm{m}$, separation $\PoseBaseline\,\mathrm{m}$ and azimuth $\PoseAz$ degrees. With a free baseline, the absolute scale remains conditional on the $\InputsFloorOffsetPrior\,\mathrm{m}$ floor-offset prior, search bounds and reconstruction objective. The pose was not chosen by agreement with the on-site beam heights.

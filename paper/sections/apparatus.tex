\section{Measurement geometry}\label{sec:apparatus}
One scintillator tracker with \InputsDetectorPlanes{} planes and \InputsBarsPerPlane{} bars per plane was placed at two floor positions beneath the ceiling. Its active width was $\InputsActiveWidthCm\,\mathrm{cm}$ and layer separation $\InputsLayerGapCm\,\mathrm{cm}$, determined from the open-sky hit distribution and angular acceptance edge. The roof exposure used the same instrument as the response reference.

The retained samples contained \DataFirstTracks{} tracks over $\DataFirstHours\,\mathrm{h}$ at pos0, \DataSecondTracks{} tracks over $\DataSecondHours\,\mathrm{h}$ at pos1, and \DataSkyTracks{} tracks over $\DataSkyHours\,\mathrm{h}$ in open sky. Live time was obtained from the inter-track interval histogram; its unlabelled time unit was inferred from the acquisition rate.

The coordinate origin is pos0, $z$ points upwards and the main beams run along $y$. The pos1 pose was fitted from the angular data. The rough floor-distance estimate was not treated as an exact survey. The on-site beam bottom and depth were $\InputsMeasuredBeamBottom\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\InputsMeasuredBeamDepth\,\mathrm{m}$; their uncertainties were not supplied. Comparison assumes a common height origin, since a separate tracker-reference-to-floor offset was not calibrated.

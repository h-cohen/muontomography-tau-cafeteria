\section{Uncertainties}\label{sec:uncertainties}
Statistical uncertainties come from \UncertaintyReplicas{} Poisson resamplings of all three data sets, with nominal rows, weights, pose and voxel grid fixed. We quote the standard deviation of the resampled results; central values are the fits to the actual data.

Systematic effects were estimated by refitting with one input changed at a time: the flux normalization by $\InputsFluxScaleFraction$, multiple scattering from the Highland formula \citep{pdg2024} for muons above $\InputsMcsMomentumCut\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$, each pose coordinate and the azimuth by their uncertainties, and the background model. Table~\ref{tab:uncertainty} gives the result for the triangulated height. The scattering shift falls below the reported height precision. The flux and background variants leave the heights unchanged by construction: a common flux scale shifts all opacities uniformly, and the background variant alters only the box-fit background. The scattering displacement $\UncertaintyMcsDisplacement\,\mathrm{m}$ is conditional on the thin matched-beam fit, not the continued-array depth. These small shifts do not establish insensitivity to general response or background errors. The uncertainty is dominated by statistics and pose. The autofocus total, $\UncertaintyAutofocusZTotal\,\mathrm{m}$, is obtained in the same way.

The budget excludes absolute-scale and height-origin calibration, joint pose covariance, the vertical offset of pos1 (fixed to the floor level of pos0), uncalibrated detector-response effects and structural model bias. The continued-array depth spread holds relative centres and pose fixed and is counting variation only.

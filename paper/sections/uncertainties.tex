\section{Uncertainty}\label{sec:uncertainties}
Counting uncertainty came from \UncertaintyReplicas{} Poisson replicas of both positions and their shared sky reference, with fit weights and geometry held fixed. The bootstrap standard deviation summarizes the sampled solutions, not a calibrated Gaussian confidence interval. Nominal values are fits to the observed data, not bootstrap means. Bound box-fit parameters remain optimizer diagnostics; their distributions are not physical dimension uncertainties.

Table~\ref{tab:uncertainty} separates counting spread of fit parameters from refit shifts for flux normalization, scattering, pose and background. The flux fraction was $\InputsFluxScaleFraction$; scattering used the Highland expression \citep{pdg2024} with an approximate survivor spectrum above $\InputsMcsMomentumCut\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$. The scattering scale is conditional on the nominal fitted depth; its in-beam displacement scale was $\UncertaintyMcsDisplacement\,\mathrm{m}$. Pose perturbations varied each marginal coordinate and azimuth separately. Some perturbations changed the selected local solution, so shifts need not be linear in the input. The quadrature total summarizes evaluated sensitivities and excludes uncalibrated scale, height-origin and model effects, including spatial response, joint correlations and matching-model recovery bias.

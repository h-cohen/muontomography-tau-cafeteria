\section{Three-dimensional room reconstruction}\label{sec:volume}
The joint reconstruction used \ReconstructionRows{} angular rows and \ReconstructionVoxels{} voxels, with weighted residual statistic $\ReconstructionChisqPerRow$ per row. Figure~\ref{fig:volume} will show the room in three dimensions, emphasizing the ceiling beams. The reconstructed field is effective opacity, not a unique material-density map; lateral structure is better constrained than its vertical extent.

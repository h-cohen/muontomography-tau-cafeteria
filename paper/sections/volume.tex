\section{Three-dimensional room reconstruction}\label{sec:volume}
The three-dimensional room image is a principal result. It combines response-corrected projections, fitted detector geometry and cross-position constraints in one voxel field, revealing repeated elongated ceiling structures beyond individual angular shadows or height estimates.

The joint reconstruction used \ReconstructionRows{} angular rows and \ReconstructionVoxels{} voxels, with residual statistic $\ReconstructionChisqPerRow$ per row. Figure~\ref{fig:volume} emphasizes the beams and their spatial arrangement. The shared-view check supports lateral placement; the preceding uncertainty analysis qualifies inferred dimensions. The field represents effective opacity, with vertical extent less constrained than lateral structure.

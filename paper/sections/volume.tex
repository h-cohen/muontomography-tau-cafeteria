\section{Room voxel model and coverage}\label{sec:volume}
The reconstruction contained \ReconstructionVoxels{} voxels and \ReconstructionRows{} measured angular rows, with weighted residual statistic $\ReconstructionChisqPerRow$ per row. This is a residual measure rather than a reduced chi-square with a known effective number of free parameters: regularization and the large null space prevent interpreting the voxel count as a fitted-parameter count. Position-only reconstructions were retained as holdouts alongside the joint solution.

Figure~\ref{fig:volume} shows orthogonal slices through the fitted beam region. The effective opacity distribution represents a regularized allocation of line-integrated attenuation. It supports lateral structural interpretation where rays from both positions cross, while diffuse vertical extent remains less identifiable. Brightness in a singly viewed or weakly covered voxel can reflect the solver's prior and nonnegativity rather than a resolved material boundary.

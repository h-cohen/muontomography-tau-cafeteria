\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Cosmic muons imaged the ceiling structure of a real room from two positions of one tracker. Transverse parallax placed the beam-opacity plane at $\BeamsZX\,\mathrm{m}$, and the shared-view reconstruction reproduced its lateral pattern. The free-opacity box fit did not resolve physical depth: the on-site $\InputsMeasuredBeamDepth\,\mathrm{m}$ remains an external measurement rather than a recovered dimension. The voxel image demonstrates the imaging capability, while the profile exposes the quantitative limit of the present geometry and response model.

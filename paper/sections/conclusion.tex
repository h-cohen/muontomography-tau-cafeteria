\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
The three-dimensional reconstruction is a central result: cosmic muons imaged the repeated ceiling structure of a real room from two positions of one tracker. It integrates the measured projections and cross-position geometry into a spatial view of the overhead beams. Transverse parallax placed their opacity plane at $\BeamsZX\,\mathrm{m}$, and the shared-view check supported lateral placement. A continued-array model gave exploratory conditional depth $\ArraydepthH\,\mathrm{m}$, compared with the on-site $\InputsMeasuredBeamDepth\,\mathrm{m}$. The dependence on centre geometry and background prevents an unconditional dimension claim; the image demonstrates room-scale imaging while the depth study identifies where further constraints are needed.

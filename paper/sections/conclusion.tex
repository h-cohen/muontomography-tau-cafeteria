\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Two positions of one tracker, normalized to an open-sky exposure, supported a reproducible room opacity reconstruction and complementary beam-geometry estimates. The concrete-pinned box fit recovered a bottom height of $\BeamdepthZbottom\,\mathrm{m}$ and depth of $\BeamdepthH\,\mathrm{m}$, compared with on-site values of $\InputsMeasuredBeamBottom\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\InputsMeasuredBeamDepth\,\mathrm{m}$. These comparison values were not imposed on the analysis. The discrepancy is reported together with the evaluated uncertainty budget, synthetic recovery behavior and model limitations. The voxel model and interactive viewer provide structural context, while the weak depth null space and free-baseline scale assumptions delimit the quantitative interpretation.

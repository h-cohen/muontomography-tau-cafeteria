\section{Beam-depth estimation}\label{sec:depth}
Rectangular boxes with free transverse centres, bottom $z_0$, width $w$ and depth $h$ were fitted to opacity maps independently of voxel regularization:
\begin{equation}
\lambda_i=\sum_k\kappa_k L_{ik}(z_0,w,h,x_k)+b_i.
\end{equation}
Aperture-averaged paths $L_{ik}$ and projected nonnegative beam opacities were fitted over polynomial backgrounds $b_i$. Analytic paths and common multistart bounds avoided density or survey constraints.

The flexible matched-beam fit favoured thin layers ($h=\BeamdepthH\,\mathrm{m}$) and a shallow profile edge (Fig.~\ref{fig:depth}). Count weights also reached the bound; matching-model noisy phantoms had maximum mean bias $\ValidationDepthMaxBias\,\mathrm{m}$. This fit did not resolve physical depth.

Continuing inferred beam spacing beyond the matched subset tested array completeness. Relative centres were fixed apart from a fitted common shift. This conditional model averaged transmission before taking its logarithm:
\begin{equation}
\lambda_i=b_i-\ln\left\langle\exp\left[-\sum_k(q_k/h)\ell_{ik}\right]\right\rangle_{\!A}.
\end{equation}
Here $\ell_{ik}$ is each admitted ray's path, with background constant across the aperture. Independent nonnegative column opacities $q_k$, bottom, depth and width remained free. The conditional estimates were bottom $\ArraydepthBottom\pm\ArraydepthBottomSigma\,\mathrm{m}$ in tracker coordinates, depth $\ArraydepthH\pm\ArraydepthHSigma\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $\ArraydepthW\pm\ArraydepthWSigma\,\mathrm{m}$. Spreads came from \ArraydepthNReplicas{} count replicas with centres, array and pose fixed; the residual statistic was $\ArraydepthChisqPerDof$ per nominal degree of freedom. The on-site bottom/depth $\InputsMeasuredBeamBottom\,\mathrm{m}$/$\InputsMeasuredBeamDepth\,\mathrm{m}$ are independent comparisons with uncalibrated height registration. These are exploratory model-dependent estimates, not unconditional dimensions.

Independent ray-box intersections checked noiseless recovery. Aperture refinement and multiple starts were consistent, but centre geometry, background and array completeness shifted depth substantially. Flux averaging alone had little effect. Survey agreement was never an optimization criterion.

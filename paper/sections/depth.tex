\section{Concrete-pinned beam-depth fit}\label{sec:depth}
Each transverse beam was represented by a rectangular box running along $y$, with a shared bottom height $z_0$, width $w$ and vertical depth $h$. Its centre coordinates were initialized by beam triangulation. The model was fitted directly to the measured opacity band, with a smooth per-position background, rather than to the regularized voxel volume. This separates the parametric depth estimate from vertical smearing in the displayed reconstruction.

Free per-beam opacity trades off against path length and hence depth. We therefore pinned the beam material to reinforced concrete with density $\BeamdepthDensity\pm\InputsConcreteDensitySigma\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$. For slant background grammage $X$ and total concrete beam path $L$, the added opacity was
\begin{equation}
\Delta\lambda=\lambda(X+100\rho_{\mathrm c}L)-\lambda(X),\qquad
\lambda(X)=-\ln\frac{I(>T_{\min}(X),\cos\theta)}{I(>T_{\min}(0),\cos\theta)}.
\label{eq:concrete}
\end{equation}
The factor converts metres to centimetres when $\rho_{\mathrm c}$ is in mass-density units. The minimum kinetic energy was obtained by adding the detector-threshold range to $X$ and inverting the tabulated concrete CSDA range \citep{groom2001,pdg2024}. The nominal spectrum was the low-energy-corrected sea-level parametrization of \citet{guan2015}; the independent spectrum of \citet{shukla2018} supplied a model-choice shift. The estimated detector kinetic-energy threshold was $\InputsDetectorThresholdGev\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Unmodified high-energy Gaisser transmission was not used in this low-energy regime.

A preliminary free-opacity fit supplied the rowwise background grammage. The pinned pass then refitted geometry and polynomial background, projecting out the linear background coefficients at each nonlinear iteration. Starts spanning shallow and deeper boxes, including the preliminary fit, were used to search for competing local solutions. The lowest objective among the evaluated fits defined the reported nominal estimate. Transmission was applied to bundle-averaged path length; averaging transmitted flux across partly covered aperture elements could produce a different edge model. This approximation, the inferred background grammage and local optimizer structure are limitations of the estimator.

The fit recovered a beam bottom of $\BeamdepthZbottom\,\mathrm{m}$ and a vertical depth of $\BeamdepthH\,\mathrm{m}$, compared with the on-site measurement of $\InputsMeasuredBeamBottom\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\InputsMeasuredBeamDepth\,\mathrm{m}$. Its fitted top was $\BeamdepthZtop\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $\BeamdepthW\,\mathrm{m}$. The measured dimensions were not imposed on the fit. The residual statistic was $\BeamdepthChisqPerDof$ per nominal degree of freedom. An offset between the fitted and measured dimensions is therefore reported as an outcome of this analysis, subject to the pose-scale and model limitations, rather than removed by recalibration to the reference.

The full estimator, including peak-derived seeds and multi-start fitting, was applied to synthetic concrete beams with the campaign rows and noise levels. The maximum absolute mean depth-recovery bias was $\ValidationDepthMaxBias\,\mathrm{m}$. These tests establish recovery for matching-model phantoms, not completeness of the real-ceiling model. The voxel beam-minus-background profile provided a separate cross-check, with full width at half maximum $\BeamdepthZprofileFwhm\,\mathrm{m}$ and analytic depth-resolution scale $\BeamdepthZprofileResolution\,\mathrm{m}$. Missing profile crossings are unmeasured, not zero-depth observations.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Two positions of one tracker produced a room-scale three-dimensional muon image with repeated lateral structure and a retrospective count-prediction advantage over a slab. Mixed angular prediction and strongly biased synthetic vertical localization delimit the result. Supporting ceiling-height and conditional box-thickness analyses provide geometric context, while physical dimensions remain conditional on registration and structural assumptions. Additional surveyed information and viewpoints are needed for independently verified depth or density.

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
The strongest result is an internally tested field of repeated lateral ceiling structure from one portable tracker and two views. The count prediction advantage is substantial, while angular prediction is mixed and shares the observed roof reference. Geometric support, fitted pose, voxel reconstruction and conditional box estimates share data and assumptions; their agreement is not independent external validation.

Vertical elongation, synthetic centroid bias, censored thin fits and reconstruction-choice sensitivity limit geometric interpretation. The pilot omits noisy null/misspecification controls and calibrated intervals. Exact numerical weighting correction removes one artifact but does not remove structural ambiguity. Surveyed detector coordinates and height origins, additional transverse viewpoints, and independent response checks would strengthen absolute scale and vertical localization. Effective opacity per unit length should not be interpreted as mass density without an energy-response/material model.
